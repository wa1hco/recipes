# Recipes

Kitchen notes. A change of pace from radios and rotators.

These are reconstructions of dishes cooked on camera, written so I can make them at home. They are **not** official recipes from the chefs, and they are **not** a substitute for the original videos.

Watch the source. The fire, the timing, and the jokes live there.

## LaoFanGu / 老饭骨

State-banquet chefs on [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@LaoFanGu). Chinese audio, Chinese hardsubs. I wrote English notes from the spoken method.
